---

UniProt features are residues and regions of a protein annotated in [UniProtKB](https://www.uniprot.org/), such as binding sites, transmembrane regions and domains. The browser shows features from UniProtKB release 2021_04 for the UniProt entry used for 3D missense constraint, and only when that entry's sequence exactly matches the protein encoded by the 3D missense constraint transcript.

Select feature types in the legend beside the structure to show them on the structure, and as rows between the 3D missense constraint track and the structure. Hover over a feature in a row to highlight its residues in the structure.

The descriptions below are adapted from UniProt's documentation. Select a feature type for UniProt's full description.

### Residues

- **[Active site](https://www.uniprot.org/help/act_site)**: residues directly involved in catalysis by an enzyme.
- **[Binding site](https://www.uniprot.org/help/binding)**: residues that interact with a chemical entity, such as a metal, cofactor, substrate or other ligand.
- **[Metal binding](https://www.uniprot.org/help/metal)**: residues that bind a metal ion. UniProt has since merged these into binding sites.
- **[Site](https://www.uniprot.org/help/site)**: single residues of interest that no other feature type describes.
- **[Disulfide bond](https://www.uniprot.org/help/disulfid)**: cysteine residues that participate in disulfide bonds. Each residue of a bond is shown separately.
- **[Glycosylation](https://www.uniprot.org/help/carbohyd)**: residues with a covalently attached glycan group (mono-, di- or polysaccharide).
- **[Lipidation](https://www.uniprot.org/help/lipid)**: residues with a covalently attached lipid group.

### Regions

- **[Transmembrane](https://www.uniprot.org/help/transmem)**: membrane-spanning regions, both alpha-helical and those of beta-barrel proteins.
- **[Intramembrane](https://www.uniprot.org/help/intramem)**: regions buried within a membrane that don't cross it.
- **[Topological domain](https://www.uniprot.org/help/topo_dom)**: the subcellular compartment where each region of a membrane-spanning protein outside the membrane is found.
- **[Domain](https://www.uniprot.org/help/domain)**: specific combinations of secondary structures organized into a characteristic three-dimensional structure or fold.
- **[Repeat](https://www.uniprot.org/help/repeat)**: repeated sequence motifs or repeated domains.
- **[Zinc finger](https://www.uniprot.org/help/zn_fing)**: zinc fingers, with their types.
- **[DNA binding](https://www.uniprot.org/help/dna_bind)**: DNA-binding domains, with their types.
- **[Coiled coil](https://www.uniprot.org/help/coiled)**: regions of coiled coil.
- **[Region of interest](https://www.uniprot.org/help/region)**: regions of interest that no other feature type describes.
- **[Motif](https://www.uniprot.org/help/motif)**: short (usually no more than 20 amino acids) conserved sequence motifs of biological significance.
- **[Signal peptide](https://www.uniprot.org/help/signal)**: an N-terminal signal peptide.
- **[Propeptide](https://www.uniprot.org/help/propep)**: parts of a protein that are cleaved during maturation or activation, and generally have no function of their own once cleaved.
- **[Transit peptide](https://www.uniprot.org/help/transit)**: the extent of a transit peptide.
